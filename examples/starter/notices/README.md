# Starter collection notice files

Notice texts the starter recipes carry where upstream repositories ship none:
the Gemma Terms of Use, Prohibited Use Policy and required NOTICE line
(Gemma 3), the Llama NOTICE lines with "Built with Llama", and the Parakeet
CC BY 4.0 attribution. Recipes pin these files by an exact commit of this
repository and their SHA-256. See `gemma/SOURCE.md` for where the Gemma texts
come from. They are not legal advice.
