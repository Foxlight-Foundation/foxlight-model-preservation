# Source of these Gemma texts

`GEMMA_TERMS_OF_USE.md` and `PROHIBITED_USE_POLICY.md` are plain-text copies
of Google's pages at https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms (last modified
2026-04-01) and https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy, retrieved on
2026-11-07. Only the HTML markup was removed; the wording is Google's.
Google's own pages are authoritative. These copies exist because Gemma 3
repositories ship no copy of the terms, while the Terms require that every
recipient receives one.
